`UNLICENSED` in package.json records that status and prevents an accidental claim
that all public code/assets are freely reusable. The package is also marked
private to prevent accidental npm publication; that does not make GitHub private.

license before adding a LICENSE file. This audit does not grant rights on their
behalf or change contribution history. See [GitHub's licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).

Dependency licenses remain applicable. IKEA names and photographs are third-party
materials, with provenance in [ikea-catalog-sources.json](ikea-catalog-sources.json)
and the fixture source record. Verification and attribution do not establish a
redistribution license. Review permissions for continued public distribution;
any future source license must explicitly exclude third-party assets it cannot
license. No claim of IKEA affiliation or endorsement is made.
